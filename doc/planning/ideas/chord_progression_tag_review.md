# Chord Progression Tag Review

Review artifact for `DevCycle007.md`.

Source of truth at review start: `app/src/main/java/com/songscaffold/app/data/PromptRepository.kt`

Current pool qualification:

- Chord Progression 1 pool: `OPEN`, `LOOP`
- Chord Progression 2 pool: `CADENTIAL`, `LIFT`, `PIVOT`
- `COLOR` is descriptive only and does not qualify a progression for either pool by itself.

Suitability tag meanings:

- `OPEN`: questioning, suspended, unresolved, or setup-oriented
- `CADENTIAL`: strongly resolves or points toward tonic resolution
- `LIFT`: brightens, widens, or intensifies the section
- `LOOP`: works as a repeating harmonic bed
- `COLOR`: uses borrowed, modal, chromatic, or distinctive harmonic color
- `PIVOT`: useful for bridge-like contrast, redirection, or sectional turn
