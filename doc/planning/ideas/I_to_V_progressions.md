# Chord Progressions Starting on I and Ending on V

These progressions from `PromptRepository.kt` begin on the I chord and resolve to V,
leaving the listener in an open, expectant state.

| Name | Category | Roman Numerals | Suitability |
|---|---|---|---|
| Circle Turnaround | Musical Theatre / Jazz | I – vi – ii – **V** | OPEN, CADENTIAL |
| 50s Progression | Classic / Standard | I – vi – IV – **V** | LIFT, LOOP |
| Descending Bass Line | Classic / Standard | I – V/7 – vi – **V** | OPEN, COLOR |
| Rhythm Changes | Musical Theatre / Jazz | I – vi – ii – **V** | OPEN, CADENTIAL |
| Chromatic Walk-Up | Musical Theatre / Jazz | I – I#dim – ii – **V** | PIVOT, COLOR |

## Notes

- **Circle Turnaround** and **Rhythm Changes** share the same roman numerals (I – vi – ii – V)
  but are listed separately; they differ in stylistic context and intended feel.
- **Descending Bass Line** passes through V/7 mid-progression before returning to V at the end.
- Two progressions end on a V-related chord but were excluded because the final chord is not
  a plain V:
  - *Suspended Loop* (I – Vsus4) ends on a suspended dominant.
  - *Pedal Loop* (I – ii/I – IV/I – V/I) ends on V over a I pedal bass.
